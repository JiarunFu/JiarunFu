# Jiarun Fu

Ph.D. Student · School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology

I am a Ph.D. student at Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Lizhong Ding. My research focuses on **statistical and causal learning, generalizable agent planning, and reinforcement learning**, with an interest in how intelligent agents reason, adapt, and generalize in complex environments.
